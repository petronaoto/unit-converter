The required area differs by a factor of 8.0. Two of the three intermediates do not move at all.
C = 346.9764 in both, and Pcf/P₁ = 0.5457 in both — because each is a function of k = 1.3 alone: C = 520·√( k·(2/(k+1))^((k+1)/(k−1)) ) and the critical ratio is (2/(k+1))^(k/(k−1)). What does move is Pcf: 98.067 psia against 53.045 psia — because that one is a ratio of P₁, and P₁ is the only pressure that changed.
And the branch is critical because P₂ sits below Pcf, not because the tool defaults there. Both cases relieve to P₂ = 0 psig, far under 98.067 and 53.045 psia respectively, so the flow chokes and the back-pressure drops out of the equation entirely. Raise P₂ past Pcf and the same card switches to the subcritical form — which is the five seconds of a review where the letter stops mattering and the assumption starts.
