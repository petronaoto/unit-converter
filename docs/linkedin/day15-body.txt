What it is not is a verdict. Three different kinds of thing get mashed together in every NPSH argument:

NPSHa is arithmetic — this card.
NPSHr is data. It exists on the vendor's tested curve for that pump at that flow, and nowhere else. No calculator can invent it.
The margin between them is judgement. Commonly 0.6–1.5 m, or a ratio of 1.1–1.35, rising steeply for hot water and for hydrocarbons near their bubble point — codified in Hydraulic Institute 9.6.1 and API 610, both licensed documents.
